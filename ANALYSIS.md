# Analytical Interpretation (Human-in-the-Loop)

After reviewing the interactive `dashboard.html` generated by our Plotly Express script, here is a detailed breakdown of our K-Means clustering results:

## 1. Strategic Archetypes
By analyzing the clusters on the Survival Time vs. Damage Dealt scatter plot, combined with the detailed tooltip data, we can define the following gaming personas:

- **Assassin**: These players map to the cluster with high average damage and high kills, but relatively low survival time. They engage early and aggressively to burst down opponents but rarely survive extended teamfights due to low durability.
- **Tank**: This cluster represents players with exceptional survival times but very low damage output and kills. They prioritize absorbing pressure and positioning over dealing damage, often surviving the longest in teamfights.
- **Support**: Falling into a high-survival and moderate-damage area, but boasting exceptionally high assists. They stick with their squad, providing utility, heals, and crowd control.
- **Mage**: This cluster groups players who have lower survival times and output less consistent damage, indicating squishy backline casters who might be struggling with positioning or getting picked off early.
- **Fighter**: The central cluster represents well-rounded brawlers. They have moderate survival times and secure a fair amount of damage and kills, indicating a balanced frontline playstyle that mixes offense and defense.

## 2. Feature Impact & Normalization
- **Handling Normalization**: In our data pipeline, we utilized Scikit-Learn's `StandardScaler` to transform our numeric features (Survival Time, Damage, Kills, Assists) so they maintain a mean of ~0 and a variance of 1.
- **Why Scaling is Mandatory**: K-Means clustering fundamentally relies on geometric distance (specifically Euclidean distance) to assign data points to the nearest centroid. If we skipped normalization, features with inherently massive numeric scales (like `Average_Damage_Dealt` which ranges in the thousands) would completely overpower features with smaller numeric scales (like `Kills_Per_Match` which ranges from 0 to 10). By scaling the data, we ensure every feature contributes equally to the distance calculation, leading to accurate, multidimensional playstyle clusters.
- **Data Integrity**: We completely isolated the string-based `Player_ID` from the input matrix before training to guarantee that it did not skew distance calculations as an arbitrary categorical value.
